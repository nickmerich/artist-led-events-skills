# Press activation by market

For a producer running events in more than one city. One home market, one or more event markets, and a national layer. Use with `press-and-media` for the writing and proof rules, and `recap-and-sponsor-report` for post-event numbers.

## Before any market

- [ ] Proof table built; every number traced to an organizer record
- [ ] Press contact mailbox tested with an external send and reply
- [ ] Artist quote requests sent; no music or national pitch until one clears
- [ ] Photo set cleared: no unconsented faces, no children identifiable
- [ ] Outlet log started: outlet, market, route, source URL, date, status, next date

## Home market (where the weekly ritual lives)

Reason to pitch: a recurring, attendable thing. Reporters can show up on Saturday.

- [ ] Local release once per season, not per event
- [ ] Pitch the arts or features desk separately from the news desk; they are different doors
- [ ] Offer a visit before a story
- [ ] After each notable run: a four-line note with the count and one cleared photo to outlets already pitched, only if something changed
- [ ] Local podcasts: accept every reasonable invite; answer scheduling questions the same day

## Event market (a city you produce in, but do not live in)

Reason to pitch: a date. No date, no pitch.

- [ ] Wave 1, when the permit or venue is confirmed in writing: the hyperlocal outlet whose beat is that neighborhood, plus the tech or business outlet if the format has a business angle
- [ ] Wave 2, seven days out: city news, events desks, free dailies, the local Axios by replying to its newsletter
- [ ] Wave 3, day after: recap with counts and cleared photos to everyone who replied, plus one listing-style note to those who did not
- [ ] Return events: one follow-up to the original list with "returns [date]" in the subject, once the date is confirmed

## National layer

Reason to pitch: a trend story that just ran, and a cleared artist quote.

- [ ] Find the trend coverage from the last 30 days; name the bylines
- [ ] Write the counterexample in one sentence
- [ ] Pitch music press only with the cleared quote; pitch running press with the format and counts; pitch business press with the producer's model
- [ ] One follow-up after seven days, with new information; then stop

## Weekly review (15 minutes)

| Question | Action |
|---|---|
| Any reply unanswered more than 24 hours? | Answer it before anything else |
| Any scheduled recording or call this week? | Prep one page: counts, dates, two stories, what to leave out |
| Any gate cleared (permit, venue, quote)? | That is your reason to send the next wave |
| Anything published? | Log it by category: feature, brief, listing, mention |

## Stop rules

- No gate cleared in four weeks: no sends that month. Do the work that clears the gate.
- Outlet has not replied after two contacts across two waves: leave it for the next event.
- Anyone asks for a claim you cannot prove: say so, offer the number you can defend.
