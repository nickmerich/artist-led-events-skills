# Questions for each authority

Ask in writing. Save the answers.

1. Do we need a permit or written approval for [format] with [N] people on [date]?
2. What triggers a permit: size, promotion, fees, sales, amplified sound, food or drink, road use?
3. Who can be the requester? Does it need to be a nonprofit?
4. Lead time? Application fee, deposit, per-person fees?
5. Insurance limits and exact additional insured wording?
6. Limits on events per month or season? Blackout dates?
7. Rules on sales, sampling, giveaways, cups, signage, chalk, flags, tents?
8. Restroom and trash requirements?
9. Parking and crowd flow requirements?
10. Police, EMS, or traffic control requirements?
11. What happens if we need to cancel or reschedule for weather?
12. Who is the day-of contact?
