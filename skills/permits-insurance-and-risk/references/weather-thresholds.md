## Reference points

- **Lightning:** The National Weather Service says organizers should have a lightning plan and follow it without exception, and wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder before resuming. Small shelters, dugouts, and tents are not safe. https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-sports
- **Heat:** The ACSM's 2007 road race guidance used WBGT flags with a black flag above 82°F (28°C) to cancel or recommend withdrawal; the 2023 consensus statement updated thresholds by region and risk group. Research on unacclimatized marathoners in northern latitudes suggests much lower do-not-start levels for community fields. Set your own conservative levels. https://www.sportsdestinations.com/management/health/acsm-heat-safety-guidelines-38034 · https://www.amssm.org/the-acsm-exchange-lecture-p-26.html
