## If someone goes down
1. Medical lead or nearest trained person responds; others clear space.
2. 911 caller calls with exact address and access point.
3. AED brought immediately if unresponsive.
4. Ambulance meeter goes to the access point.
5. Event lead decides whether to pause the event.
6. Log the incident.
