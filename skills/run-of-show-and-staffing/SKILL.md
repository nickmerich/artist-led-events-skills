# Run of Show and Staffing

## Step 1: Roles

Every event needs these roles filled by named people. One person can hold more than one at small events, except the medical lead and the event lead, who should not be doing anything else.

| Role | Does |
|------|------|
| Event lead | Owns the run of show, makes non-safety calls, only person who changes the plan |
| Medical lead | Medical response, nothing else |
| Weather decision owner | Monitors and makes the weather call (can be the event lead) |
| Talent handler | Artist arrival, timing, exits, protects their time |
| Check-in lead | Waivers, count, walk-ups |
| Welcome host(s) | Greets first-timers, pairs people up |
| Group / station / court leaders | Run the activity (ratios from the sport reference) |
| Sweep(s) | Last person in every group |
| Marshals | Crossings, turns, crowd flow |
| Partner liaison | Partner setup, deliverables, photos for the report |
| Content lead | Shot list, consent, approvals |
| Load-in / load-out | Setup, cleanup, leave it cleaner than you found it |

Staffing counts come from the sport reference's starting ratios, confirmed with the venue and medical lead.

## Step 2: Role cards

One card per role (`assets/role-card.md`): what you do, where you stand, when you're needed, who you report to, what to do if X goes wrong, radio channel or group text.

## Step 3: Run of show

Use `assets/run-of-show.md`. Work backward from the start time. Include:
- Load-in, partner setup, signage, sound check
- Crew briefing (15 minutes, always)
- Check-in open
- Welcome and safety briefing to participants (weather plan, route or rules, sweep, how to get help)
- Warm-up
- Activity blocks with exact times
- Artist moments with exact times and the handler's cues
- Partner moments
- Hang / post-event
- Load-out and site check
- Hard stop

Every line: time, what happens, who leads, where, what's needed.

## Step 4: Communications

- One channel for the crew (radio or a group text), one person who speaks for the event publicly.
- Pre-written messages for: delay, cancellation, lost participant, emergency.

## Step 5: Crew briefing (send 2 days out, repeat on site)

Who we are and why, the run of show, your role card, the emergency plan, the weather plan, and the one rule: nobody gets left behind.
