# Sponsor Outreach and Close

## Step 1: Build the list in rings

1. **Warm:** people who already know you or the artist. Existing partners, former colleagues at brands, local businesses you're a customer of.
2. **Local fit:** businesses in the event's city that would genuinely be useful on the day.
3. **Endemic national:** brands in the sport's natural partner categories (see the sport reference) with an active grassroots, club, or creator program. Look for public application forms and named community or grassroots owners.
4. **Artist-adjacent:** brands the artist's audience loves, after the conflict check.

For each prospect record: category, why them, the specific inventory you'd offer, contact path (named person beats generic inbox), relationship strength, conflict check status, and next action.

Work warm first. One warm yes becomes proof for every cold ask.

## Step 2: Write the proposal

One page (`assets/one-page-proposal.md`):
- The event in two sentences.
- Who will be there, with honest numbers and labels.
- The specific ask: one inventory option and one price. Offer the menu only if they ask.
- What they get, as deliverables with dates.
- Proof: past events and their verified results. If this is a first event, say so and offer a smaller pilot price.
- The deadline and why it's real (production lead time, print date).

## Step 3: The email

Short. From a real person's inbox, not a marketing tool, for first touches.

```
Subject: [Artist] x [Brand]: [one-line event]

Hi [Name],

[One sentence on why them, specific to their brand or program.]

[Artist] is [doing the hard thing] with [who] in [city] on [date]. We're producing it: [what we handle].

We'd love [Brand] to [specific useful role], [price or "covered with product"]. That gets you [two concrete deliverables].

Worth a 15-minute call this week? One-pager attached.

[Name]
```

Never say the artist is confirmed unless the term sheet shows it. Never imply other partners have signed if they haven't.

## Step 4: Follow up

- Day 4 to 5: one line, add one new useful detail (venue confirmed, registration opened).
- Day 10 to 12: last note, offer a smaller option or a later event.
- Then stop and log it. Silence isn't a no forever, but it's not a pipeline either.

## Step 5: Close

A yes in email is a start. Before any logo appears:
- Signed agreement or written order covering the checklist in `assets/partner-agreement-checklist.md`.
- Invoice issued with payment terms. Track paid, not just signed.
- Final conflict check against the artist's deals.

## Pipeline honesty

Track stages: Researched → Contacted → Replied → Call held → Proposal sent → Verbal yes → Signed → Invoiced → Paid. Report each stage count separately. Pipeline dollars are never revenue.
