# Partner agreement checklist

For lawyer review. Every line should be in the signed document.

- [ ] Parties, event name, date, location, and what happens if the date moves
- [ ] Exact inventory and deliverables, with quantities and dates
- [ ] Cash amount, product value, payment schedule, invoice terms
- [ ] Category exclusivity (if granted) and its limits
- [ ] Logo and name use: who approves what, response windows
- [ ] Artist approval of partner association (if artist's name is used)
- [ ] Content: what's delivered, usage rights, duration, paid usage excluded unless stated
- [ ] Participant data: no list transfer; opt-in only, with separate consent
- [ ] Product handling: delivery date, storage, leftover product, allergens and labeling
- [ ] Venue and permit compliance (sampling, sales, giveaways)
- [ ] Insurance: partner's own COI if they staff or bring equipment
- [ ] Cancellation: weather, safety, artist, partner, producer; refunds and credits
- [ ] Recap report contents and delivery date
- [ ] Morals or conduct clause (both directions)
- [ ] Tax-deductibility language only if approved by the fiscal sponsor
