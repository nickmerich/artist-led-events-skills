# Content Capture and Rights

## Step 1: Shot list from the story

Start from the concept line. Capture the moments that prove it: the open door, strangers becoming a crew, the artist doing the hard thing alongside people, the shared total.

Must-get list (adapt per sport):
- Arrival and check-in, faces not just backs
- Welcome briefing, the crowd from the front
- Every level taking part, especially the open-door group
- The artist moment
- The connection moment (people talking, partners counting reps, groups at the turnaround)
- The finish or the collective number
- Partner deliverables as listed in signed agreements
- The hang after

## Step 2: Consent

- Photo/video consent is a separate opt-in at registration. Give non-consenting participants a visible marker (colored wristband or sticker) and brief the crew to avoid featuring them.
- Minors: guardian consent, and avoid identifying minors in published content unless guardians explicitly agree.
- Close-up features of a person in published content: confirm with that person before posting.
- Medical incidents: never captured or posted.

## Step 3: Artist approvals

Posts featuring the artist follow the approval terms in the artist term sheet. Send selects in one batch with a response window.

## Step 4: Music in video

Posting video with a song needs sync rights for the composition and the recording, not just the event's performance license. Options: the artist's own music with written permission from whoever owns the publishing and master, platform-licensed music used inside the platform's own tools (follow the platform's terms; this doesn't cover partner ads), or licensed production music. Partner paid ads need their own clearance.

## Step 5: Delivery

Agree in advance: number of selects, edits, formats, delivery dates, and usage window. Partners get exactly what the agreement says. Track in `assets/shot-list.md`.
