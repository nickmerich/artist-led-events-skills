# Cycling reference

## Formats that work
| Format | Shape | Length | Good size |
|--------|-------|--------|-----------|
| No-drop social ride | Together | 60 to 120 min | 10 to 100 |
| Gravel or trail ride | Together | 1.5 to 4 hours | 10 to 60 |
| Indoor ride set with DJ or artist | Together (stationary) | 45 to 60 min | Venue bikes |
| Ride-to-the-show | Together, ends at a venue | 30 to 60 min | 20 to 200 |

## Levels and adaptations
- **Open door:** short flat loop, slow group, e-bikes welcome, loaner bikes if a partner provides them.
- **Steady:** standard route with regroup points.
- **Push:** longer or hillier route that rejoins at the end.
- **Adaptive:** handcycles and trikes on suitable routes; indoor format for anyone not comfortable on roads.
- **No-drop means no-drop:** the group waits at every regroup point.

## Venue and permission
- Public roads: riders follow traffic law; large group rides may need a city special events or parade permit and police coordination for intersections. Ask early.
- Trails: check e-bike and group rules.
- Indoor: studio or venue with its own bikes and liability terms.

## Staffing starting points (ASSUMPTION)
| Role | Starting ratio | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Ride leader | 1 per 10 to 12 riders | Sets pace, calls hazards |
| Sweep | 1 per group | Carries pump, tube, multi-tool, first aid |
| Corner marshals | Key turns | |
| Mechanic | 1 at start for pre-ride checks; ideally a support vehicle for longer rides | |
| Medical | Named lead; more for longer or remote routes | |

## Equipment
- Producer: pumps, basic tools, spare tubes, first aid, route on a shareable map, regroup point list.
- Participant: helmet (required, no exceptions), working bike, lights front and rear if any part is in low light, water.

## Safety specifics
- Helmets mandatory. Pre-ride bike check (brakes, tires, quick releases).
- Ride briefing covers hand signals, calling out hazards, single-file rules, and what to do at a regroup.
- Crashes and road traffic are the main risks. Choose routes with low traffic and few left turns.

## Scoring and standards
- Untimed. Collective miles is a good shared number.

## The artist moment
- Leads the ride out, plays the post-ride set, or curates the ride playlist (licensing applies if played publicly).

## The connection moment
- Regroup stops with a reason to talk: a viewpoint, a snack stop, a mural.

## Natural partner categories
Bike shops, bike brands, helmets, lights, e-bike companies, city bike share, coffee, nutrition, audio.

## Content moments
Rollout, the pack on a scenic stretch, regroup stop, arrival at the finish venue.

## Sanctioning bodies or insurers worth knowing
USA Cycling offers permits and insurance for some ride types; local advocacy groups often know the city process.
