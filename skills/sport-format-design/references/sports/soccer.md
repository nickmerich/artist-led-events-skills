# Soccer reference

## Formats that work
| Format | Shape | Length | Good size |
|--------|-------|--------|-----------|
| Small-sided pickup (5v5 to 7v7), rotating | Games | 2 to 3 hours | 30 to 120 per field |
| Futsal or indoor small-sided | Games | 90 to 120 min | 20 to 60 per court |
| Skills stations (passing, juggling, shooting) | Stations | 60 to 90 min | 20 to 100 |
| Collective juggles or collective goals | Collective total | 30 to 60 min | Any |

## Levels and adaptations
- **Open door:** stations and the collective juggle count; walking soccer.
- **Steady:** casual small-sided games, mixed teams.
- **Push:** a faster game field, same status.
- **Adaptive:** walking soccer (no running, low contact) is an established format for older and returning players.

## Venue and permission
- Park fields need a permit and often have season and field-rest rules. Turf facilities rent by the hour with COI requirements.
- Lights, restrooms, and parking matter more than you think.

## Equipment
- Producer: balls, pinnies, small goals or cones, first aid with ice.
- Participant: cleats or turf shoes (confirm what the surface allows), shin guards recommended, water.

## Safety specifics
- Lightning: soccer fields are among the riskiest places in a storm. Know the shelter and walk time.
- Collisions, ankles, knees. Keep it no-slide-tackle in mixed games.

## Scoring and standards
- Short games, everyone rotates. Collective goals across all fields toward one total.

## The artist moment
- Plays on a mixed team, takes a penalty against the crowd's keeper, plays a halftime set.

## The connection moment
- Mixed teams, team names from the artist's catalog, post-game food together.

## Natural partner categories
Footwear, apparel, ball brands, local clubs and leagues, hydration, audio, youth programs.

## Content moments
Goal celebrations, the artist's game, the collective total reveal.
