# Basketball reference

## Formats that work
| Format | Shape | Length | Good size |
|--------|-------|--------|-----------|
| 3x3 open run, rotating mixed teams | Games | 2 to 3 hours | 24 to 96 per 2 courts |
| Shootaround + skills stations | Stations | 60 to 90 min | 20 to 80 |
| Collective makes (crowd makes 1,000 shots) | Collective total | 45 to 60 min | 20 to 150 |
| Free-throw or three-point challenge with the artist | Stations | 30 to 60 min | Any |

## Levels and adaptations
- **Open door:** shooting stations and the collective-makes total. No games required.
- **Steady:** casual 3x3 with mixed teams, games to 11 or 10 minutes.
- **Push:** competitive bracket, kept separate in status from the open run.
- **Adaptive:** wheelchair basketball station if a local adaptive program partners; lowered-hoop station for kids.

## Venue and permission
- Rec centers, school gyms, outdoor public courts. Indoor gyms usually need a rental agreement, COI, and a facility staffer on site.
- Outdoor public courts may need a park permit for organized use or amplified sound.

## Staffing starting points (ASSUMPTION)
| Role | Starting ratio | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Court captain / ref | 1 per court | Manages rotation, keeps it friendly |
| Station lead | 1 per station | |
| Check-in and team assignment | 1 per 40 | Mix teams so friends split up |
| Medical | Named lead; ice and ankle care ready | |

## Equipment
- Producer: balls (1 per 4 people at stations), pinnies, scoreboard or whiteboard, first aid with ice.
- Participant: court shoes, water.

## Safety specifics
- Ankle sprains, finger jams, and collisions are the common injuries. Clean, dry floor; wipe sweat spots.
- Set a "friendly run" rule: no hard fouls; captains can remove someone.
- Indoor heat and hydration still matter.

## Scoring and standards
- Collective makes: one shared counter on a big screen or board. Every make counts, from any spot.
- Games: winners stay on max two games, then everyone rotates, so nobody sits all day.

## The artist moment
- Plays a few games on mixed teams, takes the last shot of the collective total, or DJs the run.

## The connection moment
- Random mixed teams. Team names drawn from the artist's songs.

## Natural partner categories
Footwear, apparel, ball brands, hydration, local sneaker shops, audio, youth programs.

## Content moments
The crowd counting down the final makes, big plays, the artist on a mixed team.
