# Weighted carries reference

Farmer's walks, sandbag carries, ruck carries. The simplest strength event there is: pick it up, walk, put it down, and do it together.

## Formats that work
| Format | Shape | Length | Good size |
|--------|-------|--------|-----------|
| Collective carry (crowd carries a shared total: pounds × distance) | Collective total | 45 to 90 min | 20 to 200 |
| Carry relay in mixed teams | Stations / Games | 60 min | 20 to 100 |
| Farmer's walk distance challenge (scaled weights) | Stations | 60 min | 20 to 80 per lane setup |
| Carry + run combo (carry a lap, run a lap) | Together | 60 to 90 min | 20 to 100 |

## Levels and adaptations
- **Open door:** light implements (kettlebells, water jugs, light sandbags). Every carry counts toward the total.
- **Steady:** moderate weights.
- **Push:** heavy implements, longer distance.
- **Adaptive:** seated or standing holds count toward a time-based total; partner carries.
- Offer at least four weight options per implement and let people choose. Don't assign by gender.

## Venue and permission
- Gyms, CrossFit boxes, school tracks, park lawns, parking lots. Indoor gyms bring their own equipment and liability rules.
- Surfaces: flat, even, non-slip. Avoid gravel for heavy carries.

## Staffing starting points (ASSUMPTION)
| Role | Starting ratio | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Lane judge / coach | 1 per lane | Watches form, calls drops |
| Weight station lead | 1 per implement set | Helps people choose weight |
| Counter / scorekeeper | 1 for the collective total | |
| Medical | Named lead | |

## Equipment
- Producer: implements in a range of weights, cones for lanes, measuring wheel, chalk optional, scoreboard.
- Participant: flat shoes, water.

## Safety specifics
- Lifting the implement off the ground is where backs get hurt. Coach a hinge setup (flat back, hips back) before anyone lifts.
- Drop rule: if grip goes, set it down under control. Never catch a falling implement.
- Clear the landing zone around lanes. Feet stay away from implements.
- Minors: lighter implements only, guardian present.

## Scoring and standards
- **Collective total:** pounds carried × distance, or simply total meters carried. One shared board.
- **A carry counts** when the implement is lifted under control, carried the marked distance without being set down, and set down under control. Set downs mid-lane: count the distance to the set-down point.

## The artist moment
- Carries the last leg of the collective total, or carries alongside a different participant each round.

## The connection moment
- Partner carries, team relays, the crowd counting the last meters.

## Natural partner categories
Gyms, strength equipment brands, grip and chalk, protein and nutrition, footwear, physical therapy, farms and local food (farmer's walk tie-in).

## Content moments
The big board counting up, faces in the last meters, the artist's carry.
