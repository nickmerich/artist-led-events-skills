# Pull-ups reference

The goal is a shared number. 500 pull-ups as a group is a format where a first-ever assisted rep matters as much as a set of 20.

## Formats that work
| Format | Shape | Length | Good size |
|--------|-------|--------|-----------|
| Group-total challenge (e.g., 500 pull-ups) | Collective total | 45 to 90 min | 10 to 100 per rig setup |
| Pull-up ladder in small groups | Stations | 45 to 60 min | 10 to 60 |
| Four-week pull-up progression ending in a group test | Challenge window | 4 weeks + event | Any |

## Levels and adaptations
Every level counts toward the shared total, 1 rep = 1 rep.
- **Open door:** dead hangs (count seconds toward a hang total), ring or bar rows at an easy angle, band-assisted reps.
- **Steady:** band-assisted or jumping negatives, partial strict reps.
- **Push:** strict pull-ups.
- **Adaptive:** seated rows or band pulldowns count toward the total.
- Count assisted and strict reps in the same total. If you show separate tallies, never rank them.

## Venue and permission
- Gyms, parks with calisthenics bars, portable rigs. Portable rigs must be assembled per manufacturer instructions and checked by a competent person before use.
- Confirm load ratings for any bar, rig, or playground structure. Playgrounds are often not rated or permitted for this.

## Staffing starting points (ASSUMPTION)
| Role | Starting ratio | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Bar coach / judge | 1 per 2 bars | Counts reps, coaches scaling, spots dismounts |
| Band and scaling station | 1 | Helps people pick an assist |
| Counter / scorekeeper | 1 for the collective total | |
| Medical | Named lead | |

## Equipment
- Producer: bars or rig with known load rating, bands in several strengths, boxes or step for reach, chalk, mats under bars, scoreboard.
- Participant: water, grips optional.

## Safety specifics
- Dismounts: step down onto a box or drop with soft knees onto mats. No kipping in a crowded rig unless spacing allows.
- Shoulders and elbows: cap sets for beginners, encourage rest between turns.
- Watch for torn calluses; first aid with tape.
- Rhabdomyolysis is a known risk with very high-volume pull-up challenges in untrained people. Cap personal volume for beginners, encourage spreading reps, and brief the medical lead.

## Scoring and standards
- **Strict rep:** start from a full hang with arms straight, pull until chin is over the bar, return to full hang.
- **Assisted rep:** same range of motion with a band or partner.
- **Hang:** seconds held, converted to the total at a published rate you set in advance (for example, 10 seconds = 1 rep). Publish the rule before the event.
- One shared counter, visible to everyone.

## The artist moment
- Takes the final rep of the total, or does one rep with every participant (as their time allows).

## The connection moment
- Partners at each bar, spotting and counting for each other.

## Natural partner categories
Gyms, calisthenics equipment, grip brands, protein and nutrition, apparel, physical therapy.

## Content moments
The counter hitting the number, someone's first-ever rep, the artist's rep.
