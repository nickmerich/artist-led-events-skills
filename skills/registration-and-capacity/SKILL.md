# Registration and Capacity

## Step 1: Free, paid, or hybrid

- **Free (partner-funded):** widest door; expect lower show rates. Only announce free when partners have committed to cover costs.
- **Paid:** higher show rates; price must clear break-even at a conservative sell assumption.
- **Refundable deposit:** small fee returned at check-in or donated. Raises show rates without making it a ticket. Check the payment platform's refund costs first.

## Step 2: Allocate the spots

Split capacity on purpose so the artist's people and the existing crew both get in. Example split for 100 numbered spots (an illustration, not a rule): 70 artist community, 20 club welcome, 10 discretionary for partners, press, and people who need a hand getting in.

Numbered tickets (001 of 100) mark belonging, not finish order.

## Step 3: Plan the show rate

Release registrations above capacity only with a justified show-rate ASSUMPTION. Base it on your own past events if you have them. Free community events commonly see a meaningful share of RSVPs not show; paid events show up at higher rates. If you have no data, release to capacity and run a waitlist instead of guessing.

## Step 4: Collect only what you need

Required: name, email, emergency contact, waiver acceptance, level or group choice.
Optional, separate checkboxes: photo/video consent, partner offers opt-in, newsletter.
Never: health details beyond what the medical lead needs; never share the list with partners.

## Step 5: Reminders

- Confirmation with date, time, exact meeting spot (map link), what to bring, and the weather plan.
- 3 days before: logistics and parking.
- Day before: weather status and "can't make it? release your spot" link (feeds the waitlist).
- Morning of, if weather is borderline: go/no-go message at a time announced in advance.

## Step 6: Check-in

- QR or name lookup, one line per 75 arrivals at peak.
- Paper waiver backup.
- Walk-up policy decided in advance (allowed up to capacity, must sign waiver).
- Live count visible to the event lead.

## Step 7: Count honestly

Report separately: registered, checked in, finished (if tracked), first-timers, returned within 30 days. Check-ins are the source of truth for attendance. Missing records are UNKNOWN, not zero.

## Test before announcing

Run a real test registration end to end: form, confirmation email, waiver stored, appears in check-in list, cancel/release works. Mark tested records clearly and delete them.
