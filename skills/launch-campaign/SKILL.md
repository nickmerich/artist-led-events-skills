# Launch Campaign

The artist's audience fills an artist-led event. Your job is to make it easy for the artist to invite them and easy for the people who come to bring a friend.

## Before anything goes out

- Venue path confirmed in writing.
- Registration tested end to end.
- Artist approved the announcement copy, title, and visuals in writing.
- Every partner named has signed.

## Six-week calendar (adjust to lead time)

| Week | Goal | Artist | Producer / club | Partners |
|------|------|--------|-----------------|----------|
| -6 | Soft demand test | Story poll or "would you come?" | Waitlist link | |
| -5 | Announce | Announcement post + story with link | Repost, email list, club channels | Approved mention |
| -4 | Why it matters | The real reason (from the concept line) | First-timer FAQ: "you can walk this" | |
| -3 | Bring someone | "Bring a friend" ask | Partner-funded spots story | Partner story |
| -2 | Proof of the doorway | Training clip of the artist at their level | Real member clip, with consent | |
| -1 | Logistics | Countdown | Full logistics, weather plan, what to bring | |
| 0 | Day of | Live stories | Live stories | |
| +1 | Recap | Thank-you post | Recap, photo gallery, next event | Partner recap |

## Asset list

Announcement graphic (square and vertical), artist announcement copy, first-timer FAQ, logistics post, partner mentions, bring-a-friend share card, day-of story frames, recap template.

## Copy rules

- Visible signup URL as text in captions, not only "link in bio."
- Lead with the experience and the people, not the partner.
- Say what the open-door option is in every announcement ("walkers welcome," "every rep counts").
- No invented urgency. "Sold out" only when it's true. "Limited spots" only with a real cap.
- No unconfirmed names: talent, partners, or special guests.
- No attendance claims beyond verified numbers.
- Avoid wording that encourages impairment, risky behavior, or pushing through pain.

## Messages to draft

- Artist announcement (in the artist's voice, for their approval)
- Producer announcement
- Email to club or list
- Partner-facing launch note
- DM template the artist's team can send to superfans
- Bring-a-friend message participants can copy and paste

Use `assets/launch-checklist.md` to track each asset through drafted → approved → scheduled → posted.
