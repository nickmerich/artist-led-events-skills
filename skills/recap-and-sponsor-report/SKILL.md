# Recap and Sponsor Report

The recap is where trust gets built or lost. Report what happened, not what you hoped.

## Step 1: Pull the verified numbers (within 48 hours)

From check-in and registration records:
- Registered, checked in, first-timers, by level or group if tracked
- Partner deliverables completed (product distributed, station hours, assets delivered)
- Incidents (count and type; no personal details)
- Collective total if the format had one

Label anything not from a record. Missing is UNKNOWN.

## Step 2: Public recap

Lead with a real moment (with permission), then the shared result, then thank-yous, then the next step (next event, weekly club, waitlist). No invented quotes. Photos from consenting participants only.

## Step 3: Sponsor report (within 7 days)

Use `assets/sponsor-report.md`. One per partner:
- What they paid for, line by line, and what was delivered
- Verified attendance numbers and how they were counted
- Photos of their activation and their product in use
- Content links and usage rights
- What we'd do better
- The renewal ask: one specific next option with a date

## Step 4: Artist debrief

What worked for them, what didn't, would they do it again, what they'd change. Settlement statement if there's a split, on the agreed date.

## Step 5: Return follow-up (day 1, 7, and 30)

- Day 1: thank-you with photos and the next event or weekly club invite.
- Day 7: one personal note to first-timers from a crew member.
- Day 30: count how many came back to anything. That's the number that tells you if the event built a crew or just a crowd.

## Step 6: Lessons log

Add to `assets/lessons-log.md`: format, verified headcount, what broke, what you'd change. Fold strong lessons back into the relevant skill.
