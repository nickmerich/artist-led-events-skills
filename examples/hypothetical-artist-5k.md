# Worked example: a hypothetical artist 5K

**Everything here is invented to show how the skills fit together.** The artist, city, partners, and numbers are not real. Labels show how a real plan would mark them.

## 1. Concept (`artist-fit-and-concept`)
- Artist: "Juniper Hale," indie singer, training for a first half marathon (ASSUMPTION for this example).
- Eligibility: current release, real practice (posts weekly runs), local pull (hometown show history), manager reachable.
- Scores: Q1 Supported, Q2 Supported, Q3 Partial (manager interested, no written role), Q4 Unknown, Q5 Partial.
- Route: Q3 Partial → **Pilot Design**. Next action: send the low-lift term sheet.
- Concept line: Juniper is running her first 5K with fans in her hometown because she's training for her first half and wants company.

## 2. Format (`sport-format-design` + `running.md`)
- Shape: Together. Out-and-back route with 1 mi walk, 5K, and 5 mi turnarounds.
- Capacity: venue 400, permit 250, insurance no stated cap, staffing 12 leaders × 12 = 144 → **capacity 144**. Plan 125 numbered spots.
- Artist moment: starts the run with the walk group, plays two songs acoustic at the finish.
- Connection moment: pace groups with named leaders; coffee after.

## 3. Deal (`artist-deal-terms`)
- Low-lift: arrive 7:15, leave 9:30. Two posts. No fee; profit after costs split 50/50 if any. Artist approves announcement and partners.

## 4. Money (`event-budget-pnl`)
Run `python3 skills/event-budget-pnl/scripts/pnl.py examples/hypothetical-budget.csv --headcount 125`. Free entry, so partner revenue must cover all costs before announcing free.

The script reports $3,150 in costs, $3,000 in partner revenue, a $150 gap, and 100% of lines still estimates. Verdict: **not ready to announce free entry.** Next actions: get written quotes for the four biggest lines, then close the gap by trimming photo scope or adding a third partner. Re-run before Gate 2.

## 5. Partners (`sponsor-inventory-and-pricing`, `sponsor-outreach-and-close`)
- Access partner funds 125 free spots. Coffee partner provides product. Conflict check: artist's manager reports an existing beverage deal, so hydration category is held for that brand or left empty.

## 6. Permission (`permits-insurance-and-risk`)
- Park department permit, 8 weeks lead time, COI with park as additional insured. Lightning and WBGT thresholds written; medical lead named.

## 7. Launch (`registration-and-capacity`, `launch-campaign`)
- 125 spots split 85 artist community / 30 club / 10 discretionary. Waitlist on. Announce at W-5 after Gate 2.

## 8. Day of and recap
- Run of show, role cards, shot list. Sponsor reports within 7 days. Count 30-day returns.
